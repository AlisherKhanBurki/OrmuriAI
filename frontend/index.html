1. Split Ergativity Sentence Formula

            

              

                Present / Future
                Subject[DIR] + Object + VerbStem[Pres]-Agr[Subj] + {بُو / سُو}
              

              
Verb agrees with the SUBJECT in person and number. Particle بُو for present/habitual, سُو for future.

              
              

                Past Transitive
                Agent-Clitic + Object[ABS] + VerbStem[Past]-Agr[Obj(Gender, Number)]
              

              
Verb agrees with the OBJECT in gender and number. Agent is marked by an enclitic hosted on the first constituent or ال (al).
